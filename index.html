The problem

Booking one private lesson shouldn't take two days of texts

Your private lessons are your business: your diary, your rates, your students' notes, in your pocket, not in the club's system.

Assumption Who we think it's for, before launch.

Who pays

The coach. Black and brown belts in Ireland who teach paid one-to-one sessions on their club's mats as their own side business, whether you part-own the club or coach on someone else's timetable. You pay for it yourself. It's your tool, not the club's.

Who uses it every day

You and your private students. Mostly adults fitting training around a job, who want a time, a price and a reminder of what they drilled.

Not the buyer

The club owner. Side Control is built for the individual coach. A club-wide version is Planned

How it works today

WhatsApp, memory and an awkward chat about money

Assumption From our own experience on the mats, not a survey.

  1. The coach posts a reminder in the club WhatsApp group.
  2. Students DM, or ask after class.
  3. Times ping-pong back and forth until something sticks.
  4. Sometimes there's a check-in text the day before.
  5. The price is agreed verbally, then paid in cash or Revolut afterwards.
  6. What was covered lives in the student's memory, or a note on their phone.

How Side Control works

Three steps, no back-and-forth

  1. 1

    Coach shares open times

    Set your weekly windows once. They're split into one-hour slots, and anything in the past drops off on its own.

  2. 2

    Student taps a slot and sees the price

    Their own rate, shown before they book and locked to the booking. If they've bought a pack of 10 or 20, it says “1 session from your pack” instead. Once it's booked, the slot disappears for everyone else. (In this demo that works between tabs on one device; across phones it needs the shared server: Planned)

  3. 3

    After the session: paid and notes logged

    Mark it Cash or Revolut, tap in what you covered, add something to practise and a video link if you like, and it lands in the student's Next Stripe log.

    Each month, the Money tab adds up what you took in Cash and Revolut, the late-cancel fees you recovered, what cancellations cost you and what's still owed. Download it as a CSV.

Built for one thumb, between classes: big buttons, one screen per job, nothing to install. Bookings land in the app, not in another WhatsApp chat.

How students get in

You share your booking link (on WhatsApp, or in the club group). Students open it on their phone: no app to install, no account to create. They pick their name the first time.

Reality check

We tested it

An informal, hands-on test of the demo before launch.

Who tried it
A club owner who teaches private lessons, another coach, and two students testing at the same time.
What they tried
Booking as students, the coach view, and two students booking the same slot at once in separate browsers.
What broke
Two students both booked and confirmed the same slot: a double booking.
What confused them
  • No tutorial.
  • How students get access.
  • The blue accent colour: a coach said a blue belt was “a bit insulting” for an app aimed at black belts.
  • Where data is stored.
  • No monthly view of money.
What we changed
  • A last-second slot check, plus live updates between tabs. Across phones it needs a shared server Planned
  • A “How students get in” section.
  • A black belt colour scheme.
  • A “Your data” section.
  • A “Try this” hint strip in the demo.
  • Packs of 10 or 20 sessions, with sessions remaining.
  • Homework and video links in the notes.
  • A monthly money summary with a CSV download.

This demo In the demo

Everything you do here is saved only on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Reset sample data wipes it.

When it launches Planned

  • The coach owns their students' data. Side Control stores it for them.
  • Stored on EU servers.
  • Only what's needed: name, a contact, belt, rate, bookings, payment status and technique notes.
  • No card details, because payment happens outside the app.
  • A prompt reminding coaches not to record injuries or medical details in notes.
  • Inactive students deleted after 2 years, unless the coach keeps records for tax.
  • A privacy notice for students, and a data processing agreement for coaches.

Not legal advice: we'll have this reviewed before launch.
