The problem

Booking one private lesson shouldn't take two days of texts

Your private lessons are your business: your diary, your rates, your students' notes, in your pocket, not in the club's system.

Who pays

The coach. Black and brown belts in Ireland who teach paid one-to-one sessions on their club's mats as their own side business, whether you part-own the club or coach on someone else's timetable. You pay for it yourself. It's your tool, not the club's.

Who uses it every day

You and your private students. Mostly adults fitting training around a job, who want a time, a price and a reminder of what they drilled.

Not the buyer

The club owner. Side Control is built for the individual coach. A club-wide version is Planned

How it works today

WhatsApp, memory and an awkward chat about money

  1. The coach posts a reminder in the club WhatsApp group.
  2. Students DM, or ask after class.
  3. Times ping-pong back and forth until something sticks.
  4. Sometimes there's a check-in text the day before.
  5. The price is agreed verbally, then paid in cash or Revolut afterwards.
  6. What was covered lives in the student's memory, or a note on their phone.

What's already out there

Gymdesk

Full gym-management software. It can run private lessons as bookable sessions, with per-instructor calendars and packs, but inside the club's account, set up by the club.

Checked 27 Oct 2026

BJJNote

A student training journal that logs private lessons and each instructor's techniques, but the student types it all in themselves and there's no booking.

Checked 27 Oct 2026

Our view

One covers booking, the other covers notes. Nothing we found joins the two up from the coach's phone.

How Side Control works

Three steps, no back-and-forth

  1. 1

    Coach shares open times

    Set your weekly windows once. They're split into one-hour slots, and anything in the past drops off on its own.

  2. 2

    Student taps a slot and sees the price

    Their own rate, shown before they book and locked to the booking. Once it's booked, nobody else can take that slot.

  3. 3

    After the session: paid and notes logged

    Mark it Cash or Revolut, tap in what you covered, and it lands in the student's Next Stripe log.

Built for one thumb, between classes: big buttons, one screen per job, nothing to install. Bookings land in the app, not in another WhatsApp chat.
